Introducing ZaviExito Global Trade

Showcasing India’s Timeless Artistry to the World

ZaviExito Global Trade is a trusted exporter of Indian handicrafts, offering premium marble decor, artisan jewellery, and handwoven carpets. We work with skilled artisans to deliver authentic, customizable products for global buyers. Every piece reflects heritage, craftsmanship, and modern elegance.

Our Core Values
  • Authenticity – Genuine handcrafted products sourced from India’s artisan clusters.
  • Quality Assurance – Export-standard finishing and packaging for global buyers.
  • Customization – Tailor-made designs as per client requirements.
  • Certification – Company led by a DGFT-Certified (MOOC) xport Professional under the Niryat Bandhu Scheme, Ministry of Commerce, Government of India.

Introducing About Xito Footwear

Designed for comfort in every moment
